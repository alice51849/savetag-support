What a read-later bookmark manager should do

  • Save from anywhere, not only from one browser.
  • File things for you, because nobody tags 40 links by hand.
  • Find by idea, since you rarely remember the exact title.
  • Bring saves back, so your read-later list shrinks instead of growing.
  • Let you leave, with an export so your saves are never locked in.

How to save articles and links with SaveTag

  1. Share into SaveTag. From any app with a share sheet, pick SaveTag and go straight back to what you were doing.
  2. Or quick-save from the clipboard. Copy a link and save it in one tap.
  3. Let it fill in the details. Title, site and preview are pulled in for you.
  4. Let tags write themselves. Every save is sorted into a topic the moment it lands, on your device. Add custom tags if you want your own system.
  5. Search by meaning. Search runs across titles, notes, tags and sources, so a vague memory is enough.
  6. Use Rediscover daily. A small handful of unread saves each day, with a goal ring and a streak.

Privacy and purchase

No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Your saves never leave your device. Tagging runs on Apple's on-device text intelligence; it is not a chatbot and never writes content for you. Without a purchase you get every feature with your latest 5 saves. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited saves, custom tags, Markdown export and backup, and Family Sharing. No subscription, ever.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save an article from a browser, a video app or a social app?

Open the share sheet in that app and choose SaveTag. Any app with a share sheet works, and you can also save a copied link from the clipboard.

Does SaveTag organize bookmarks automatically?

Yes. Each save is tagged by topic the moment it lands, using Apple's on-device text intelligence.

What if I don't remember the title?

Search covers titles, notes, tags and sources, so searching for the idea is usually enough.

Are my saves uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no account and no cloud; your saves stay on your iPhone.

Is SaveTag a subscription?

No. You can use every feature with your latest 5 saves; a one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited saves. There is no subscription.

Can I export my bookmarks?

Yes, Markdown export is included with the one-time purchase, so your saves are never locked in.

SaveTag is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple. On-device tagging uses Apple's text intelligence; availability depends on your device and iOS version.
