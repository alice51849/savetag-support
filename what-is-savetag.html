What is SaveTag?

SaveTag is an app for saving links to read or use later. Share a link from any app with the share sheet (or quick-save it from the clipboard) and SaveTag fills in the title, site and preview, then tags it on your device into topics such as food, recipes, travel, shopping, style, work and learning. Search looks across titles, notes, tags and sources, so a rough memory is enough to find a save again, and Rediscover brings back a small handful of unread saves each day.

Who is SaveTag for?

People who send themselves links all day and lose them in chats, notes and screenshots: shoppers building a wishlist, cooks collecting recipes, travellers planning a trip, parents keeping advice and designers keeping references. It suits anyone who wants a read-later list that actually gets shorter, and anyone who prefers that saves stay on the phone: there is no account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking.

How does SaveTag charge?

SaveTag is free to download. Without a purchase you get every feature with your latest 5 saves. SaveTag Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that adds unlimited saves, custom tags, Markdown export and backup, and Family Sharing. The listing states there is no subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is SaveTag different from other bookmark and read-later apps?

Based on what the listing describes: tagging happens automatically on the device using Apple's on-device text intelligence (it is not a chatbot and never writes content for you); search matches the idea rather than the exact words; Rediscover, with a goal ring and a streak, is built to make the list shrink; saves never leave the device; and Markdown export means your saves are never locked in. It also has a Home Screen widget, Siri shortcuts and 50 languages.
